# 💍 Bride or Groom? 💍

*For each question, guess whether the answer is the **Bride** or the **Groom**. Write **B** or **G** next to each one. Don't trust your gut — some of these are designed to fool you. 😉*

---

## 😴 Mornings & Sleep

1. Who is always running late?
2. Who wakes up earlier in the morning?
3. Who hits snooze the most times?
4. Who falls asleep first during a movie?
5. Who makes the coffee or tea for the other one?

## 📱 Phone & Tech

6. Who spends more time on their phone?
7. Who has more open tabs in the browser?
8. Who has more screenshots they'll never look at again?
9. Who has more random photos of food in their camera roll?
10. Who watches more videos at 2x speed?
11. Who has a "favorites" playlist of 4 songs on repeat for 3 years?
12. Who has the most wholesome YouTube recommendations?
13. Who has watched the same comfort show 7 times?

## 🗣️ Social & Personality

14. Who talks more to strangers?
15. Who's the loud one you can hear from across the room?
16. Who tells the longer stories?
17. Who's the fierce competitor that absolutely refuses to lose?
18. Who replies to texts faster?
19. Who's the daredevil — first to say "let's do it" to anything risky?
20. Who googles the answer mid-argument to prove a point?
21. Who takes longer to actually leave after saying "okay, let's go"?
22. Who says "I told you so"?
23. Who waves back at someone who was actually waving at the person behind them?
24. Who gives the warmest hellos and goodbyes?
25. Who remembers everyone's name at the party?

## 🍴 Food & Household

26. Who controls the thermostat?
27. Who's the real chef of the house, doing most of the cooking and baking?
28. Who keeps more "I'll use it someday" stuff?
29. Who's more likely to eat the last slice and deny it?
30. Who eats more?
31. Who watches the recipe video but will never, ever make it?
32. Who says "I'm not hungry" then eats half your plate?
33. Who "just has a bite" of dessert and finishes the whole thing?
34. Who saves the best bite for the other person?
35. Who finishes their meal and then eyes the other's plate?

## 🛒 Out & About

36. Who goes in for milk and comes out with $80 of "essentials"?
37. Who pushes the shopping cart like it's a Formula 1 race?
38. Who confidently presses "depart" then asks which way to turn?
39. Who's dressed and waiting by the door 30 minutes early?

## 🦸 Everyday Heroes & Brain Quirks

40. Who's the official spider-relocation officer?
41. Who untangles every charger, necklace, and pair of headphones?
42. Who remembers song lyrics but not why they walked into the room?
43. Who's the human GPS for "where did I leave my…"?

## 🥰 Soft & Sweet

44. Who talks to every dog like it's a person?
45. Who waves at babies in the supermarket?
46. Who names the houseplants?
47. Who took 200 photos on the trip — and 198 were of the other person?
48. Who'll cry happy tears during the speeches?
